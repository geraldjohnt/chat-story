# Split the Rental

Status: pending
Proposed: 2026-10-09
Genre: Forced-proximity romance / workplace conflict of interest → romance with a cost
Setting: Denver International Airport → Omaha, Nebraska; 19 December 2026 – 12 January 2027
Proposed parts: 3. Part 1 is the meeting and the spark, part 2 is the conflict of interest revealed,
and part 3 is the choice and what it costs. The drive itself happens offscreen, in person, so the
texts carry the before and after. A fourth part would just pad the waiting.

## Premise
Two strangers stranded by a blizzard split the last rental car for an eight-hour drive. Weeks later,
he finds out she's the consultant hired to restructure his department, and that she knew for six days
before telling him.

## Synopsis
When every flight to Omaha is cancelled, logistics manager Femi takes the last SUV at the rental desk.
The woman behind him in line, Claire, offers to split the car and the driving. The texts before
departure are logistics and wary jokes. His sister Tolu demands a photo "in case she's a serial
killer". After the drive, the texts don't stop: home safe?, a Christmas-morning photo, a New Year's
Eve call. Claire says she's in Omaha in January "for a client" she can't name. Then a 4 January
all-staff email at Halvorsen Freight welcomes Lindqvist Partners for an "operational efficiency
review", led by Claire Novak. Femi's team is in scope. His friend and direct report Dev asks if he's
heard anything about layoffs. Claire knew from 29 December, when she got the org chart, and hadn't
disclosed it yet.

## Main characters
- `femi` **Femi Adebayo** (36, device owner, operations manager at Halvorsen Freight, Omaha). He's
  steady and principled, protective of his team, and a little lonely since a long relationship ended.
  Voice: proper capitalisation, dry humour, sends voice-note-length thoughts as two short messages,
  👍 only to his sister.
- `claire` **Claire Novak** (34, senior consultant at Lindqvist Partners, Chicago). She's ambitious,
  funny, and honest by instinct, but she's up for partner track this cycle and this engagement is her
  shot. Voice: rapid, self-deprecating, parentheses (lots of them), "ok but", rarely uses emoji.
- `tolu` **Tolu Adebayo** (31, Femi's sister, Minneapolis). She's comic relief with real wisdom.
  Voice: 😭💀, all lowercase, "brother."
- `dev` **Dev Raman** (40, Femi's direct report and friend, two kids, mortgage). He's anxious and
  decent. Voice: "hey man", long worried messages, then "anyway".
- `halvorsen` **Halvorsen Freight (All Staff)**. The inbox/automated sender of company emails.
- `skyway` **Skyway Air**. Automated flight alerts.

## Relationships
Femi and Claire are strangers who become something real over three weeks. Femi and Tolu are close
siblings. Femi and Dev have worked together for six years, and Femi hired him. Claire reports to a
Lindqvist partner (offscreen, Margaret Holt).

## Central conflict
New feelings against professional integrity, on both sides. Claire has to choose between disclosing
the relationship (and losing the engagement, probably her promotion too) and staying silent (an
ethics breach that could taint the review of Femi's team). Femi worries that any outcome for his team
will look rigged in one direction or the other. With Dev, he has to show he didn't trade on it.

## Secrets and planned twists
- Claire knew on 29 Dec and waited six days. Evidence: her 29 Dec text shifts tone, "can we talk
  after the 4th? (nothing bad, mostly)" (part 1). She admits it in part 2.
- Claire never shared anything confidential. Femi tests this in part 2 when Dev begs him for
  information, and asks Claire flat out. She refuses, which proves her integrity in the most painful
  way.
- Claire's disclosure email to her partner. She sends Femi a screenshot of it in part 3 (shown as an
  image attachment).
- There's no villain twist. The tension is ethical, not a lie about who someone is.

## Planned ending
Romance with a cost. Claire discloses and is removed from the engagement. Her partner-track review is
pushed to next cycle. The review continues under someone else. Dev's role is consolidated into a new
position, not cut, after Femi formally argues for it on the record without any contact with
Lindqvist. Dev, rattled, admits he briefly suspected Femi and apologises. Final beat: Claire, 12 Jan:
"I'm in Omaha on the 16th. Not for work. (Do you still have the ice scraper?)" Femi: "It has your name
on it." Ending type: romantic, with a professional price paid.

## Part-by-part outline
1. **Last SUV** (19–31 Dec, then 4 Jan). A Skyway cancellation notification. A rental-counter text
   receipt with two drivers listed. Femi ↔ Claire coordinating at the curb. Femi ↔ Tolu: "she brought
   gas station sushi on a road trip." Timestamp jump: "home safe?" Christmas morning photos, the New
   Year's call. Claire's 29 Dec tone shift. Cliffhanger: a lock-screen notification on 4 Jan, the
   all-staff email "Welcome Lindqvist Partners", with Claire's name in the preview.
2. **In Scope** (4–6 Jan). Femi ↔ Claire: "six days, Claire." Her honest explanation. Dev ↔ Femi: "you
   hear anything? Priya in finance says ops is first." Femi asks Claire whether his team is on the list.
   She replies: "I can't answer that. And if you ask again I'll have to report that you asked." Tolu:
   "brother she's RIGHT though." Cliffhanger: Claire: "I haven't disclosed yet. If I do, I'm off the
   project. If I don't, I can't see you. I need you to tell me which one you can live with." Femi: "No.
   That one's yours."
3. **Disclosure** (7–12 Jan). Claire sends a screenshot of her disclosure email. Margaret's reply
   removes her. Notification Center: an all-staff update on the "review team change". Femi formally
   writes to his VP about Dev's role; Dev finds out who Claire is from office gossip, and there's a
   confrontation and an apology. The outcome email arrives. Final exchange about the ice scraper.

## Continuity considerations
Fixed facts: Flight SK 2214 DEN→OMA was cancelled 19 Dec. The rental was a white Ford Explorer from
"Front Range Car Rental". The drive was 8 h 40 min via I-76/I-80, and they stopped in North Platte. The
engagement kickoff is 4 Jan. Claire saw the org chart 29 Dec. Use plausible, non-specific consulting
ethics (a disclosure policy). Femi never receives confidential information.

## Originality assessment
Compared with 0001, 0002, 0003, and this batch's "Is This Still Ben?" and "Before 8 A.M.".
- It's the only workplace-ethics conflict, and the only story where the main couple meet in person in
  part 1.
- The secret is a short, motivated delay in disclosure, not hidden money (0001, 0003) or a child's
  scheme (0002).
- It shares 0003's "automated email as evidence" device, but only for the part 1 hook. After that, the
  conflict is driven by choices, not documents.
- It's the first male device owner.
- The ending (romance gained at a professional price) isn't used in the index.
