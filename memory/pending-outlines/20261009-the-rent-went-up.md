# The Rent Went Up

Status: pending
Proposed: 2026-10-09
Genre: Roommate drama / misunderstanding → reconciliation
Setting: Shared two-bed terraced house in Leeds, UK; late January, present day
Proposed parts: 3 — part 1 builds suspicion, part 2 escalates to a confrontation on the wrong evidence,
part 3 delivers the reveal and its fallout. A fourth part would only stretch the misunderstanding.

## Premise
Priya is convinced her roommate Callum is quietly scamming her on rent — until she finds out he has
spent four months secretly paying the landlord's increase on her behalf.

## Synopsis
After a "thanks for sorting January 👍" text from their landlord that makes no sense to her, Priya
starts noticing odd things: Callum dodging questions about the tenancy renewal, a bank notification
showing he moved more money than his share, a landlord message mentioning "the new amount". She
assumes Callum has been inflating the rent and pocketing the difference. Her sister Anjali eggs her on.
Priya confronts him in the house group chat with the landlord in it. The truth: the rent rose £140 in
October, the same week Priya was put on reduced hours, and Callum — knowing she'd refuse help —
covered her half of the increase and asked the landlord to keep it quiet. Priya has to deal with
having publicly accused a friend, and with her own pride.

## Main characters
- `priya` **Priya Nair** (27, device owner, pharmacy technician on cut hours) — proud, organised,
  hates owing anyone. Voice: full sentences, full stops, occasional dry "lol", no emoji when upset.
- `callum` **Callum Reid** (29, roommate, sound engineer) — conflict-avoidant, kind, bad liar.
  Voice: lowercase, lots of "ha", replies late at night, deflects with jokes.
- `anjali` **Anjali Nair** (31, Priya's older sister, solicitor's paralegal) — protective, suspicious,
  quick to escalate. Voice: rapid bursts of short messages, CAPS for emphasis, 👀.
- `dave` **Dave Whitlock** (58, landlord) — polite, old-fashioned. Voice: signs off "Regards, Dave",
  ellipses everywhere, thumbs-up emoji.

## Relationships
Priya and Callum met at uni, have shared the house for three years, and are close friends (no romance
plotted; Anjali teases about it once). Anjali has never liked Callum's "vibe". Dave is a decent small
landlord who likes Callum.

## Central conflict
Priya's pride and fear of being taken advantage of vs. Callum's well-meant secrecy. The more Callum
hides, the guiltier he looks.

## Secrets and planned twists
- Rent increased £140/month from October — Dave and Callum — Dave's "new amount" slip (part 1),
  standing-order notification (part 1) — revealed part 3.
- Callum is covering Priya's £70 share of the increase — Callum — bank notification of an odd amount
  £70 above his share (part 1), Callum asking Dave by text not to "mention the figure to P"
  (seen by Priya in part 3 when Dave forwards it to defend himself) — revealed part 3.
- Callum also turned down a better flat to stay — Callum — mentioned by a friend in passing (part 3).
- Misdirection: Anjali reads the extra £70 as Callum's "skim"; the evidence is real, the interpretation
  is wrong. No fact is withheld from the viewer unfairly — the clues fit both readings.

## Planned ending
Reconciliation with consequences: Priya apologises publicly in the same group chat, insists on a
repayment plan Callum reluctantly accepts, and they renew the lease together. Final beat: Anjali
texts Callum directly for the first time — "fine. you're alright." Warm, slightly funny, not saccharine.

## Part-by-part outline
1. **Thanks for Sorting January** — Lock-screen notification from Dave hooks the viewer. Priya and
   Callum chat (he deflects). Notification of Callum's transfer to the shared account. Priya ↔ Anjali
   chat: suspicion forms. Cliffhanger: Dave texts "Callum mentioned you'd prefer I didn't bring up the
   new amount… understood."
2. **The House Chat** — Priya gathers "evidence" with Anjali; Callum cancels a planned chat twice.
   Priya posts in the house group (Priya, Callum, Dave): "Can someone tell me what the rent actually
   is?" Read receipts, Callum typing… stops. Cliffhanger: Callum: "can we talk about this in person
   please" — Priya: "No. Here."
3. **The New Amount** — Dave states the real figures; Callum admits it in fragments. Dave forwards
   Callum's October text. Priya's silence (timestamps jump 3 hours). Conversation-list screenshot
   showing her unsent drafts? (no — show list with Callum's unread messages). Apology, repayment-plan
   argument, lease renewal, Anjali's closing text.

## Continuity considerations
Fix figures: old rent £1,050 (£525 each), new £1,190 (£595 each); Callum pays £665. Increase from
1 October. Story runs 24–31 January. Priya's reduced hours began the first week of October.
