# Pickup at 3:15

Status: pending
Proposed: 2026-10-09
Genre: Co-parenting drama / family secret → bittersweet
Setting: Columbus, Ohio, USA; early March, present day
Proposed parts: 4 — the story has two movements (co-parent suspicion, then the child's real
problem) that each need setup and payoff; three parts would rush the turn, five would pad it.

## Premise
A divorced mom discovers her ex's new girlfriend has been secretly picking up their daughter from
school — and assumes she's being edged out, until she learns her daughter arranged it to hide
something.

## Synopsis
Dana gets an automated school-office text confirming an "authorized adult pickup" she never
approved, on one of her custody days. Her ex-husband Marcus is evasive; his girlfriend Renée is
politely vague. Dana assumes Marcus is building a case for more custody. The fight escalates with
Dana's lawyer friend weighing in. Then a message from her daughter Lily's classmate's mom reveals
Lily has been avoiding the bus line where a group of older kids wait — and that Lily asked Renée,
whom she trusts because Renée "doesn't make a big deal", not to tell either parent. Dana and Marcus
must stop fighting each other and face why their daughter didn't come to them.

## Main characters
- `dana` **Dana Whitfield** (38, device owner, ER nurse, shifts) — fiercely protective, tired,
  quick to read threats. Voice: efficient, punctuation-correct, "K." when angry.
- `marcus` **Marcus Bell** (40, ex-husband, HVAC business owner) — defensive after a hard divorce,
  loves Lily, avoids conflict by under-informing. Voice: short replies, "👍", voice-note energy in text.
- `renee` **Renée Okafor** (34, Marcus's girlfriend of 8 months, middle-school art teacher) —
  careful, kind, caught between a promise to a child and two adults. Voice: warm, complete
  sentences, apologetic.
- `lily` **Lily Bell** (9, daughter, has a kid-tablet with iMessage) — bright, private.
  Voice: emoji strings, "ok", spelling slips, never answers questions directly.
- `tasha` **Tasha Grant** (39, Dana's friend, family lawyer) — strategic, wants receipts.
  Voice: brisk, lists, "screenshot that".
- `school` **Hamilton Elementary** (automated SMS) — template messages.

## Relationships
Dana and Marcus divorced two years ago; 50/50 week-on/week-off with set handover days. Their texting
is civil but brittle. Renée has met Lily often; Dana has met Renée twice. Tasha has known Dana since
nursing school.

## Central conflict
Dana's fear of losing her place in Lily's life vs. Marcus's and Renée's secrecy. Underneath: a child
handling bullying alone because she sees her parents as already at war.

## Secrets and planned twists
- Renée has done three pickups at Lily's request — Renée/Lily — school SMS (part 1), Lily's tablet
  message "renee can u come thursday" seen by Dana when Lily's tablet syncs to Dana's notification
  center (part 2) — revealed part 3.
- Marcus knew about one pickup and assumed Dana had agreed — Marcus — his "I thought you knew"
  slip (part 2) — revealed part 2.
- Older kids at the bus line have been taking Lily's things and mocking her — Lily — classmate's mom
  Jenna's message (part 3) — revealed part 3.
- Twist mechanism: the "custody threat" Dana builds a case around is a child's workaround, not an
  adult scheme. Clues: pickups only on Thursdays (bus-line day), Lily's sudden "I hate the bus".

## Planned ending
Bittersweet: the bullying is addressed with the school and Lily is OK, but Lily's admission that she
"didn't want to start another fight" lands hard. Dana and Marcus agree to a shared co-parenting
group chat including Renée — a functional truce, not a romance or a full friendship. Final beat:
Lily's first message in the new group: "is this the team chat 🙂". Dana keeps her lawyer's draft
email unsent.

## Part-by-part outline
1. **Authorized Adult** — Lock-screen school SMS mid-shift. Dana ↔ Marcus: evasive. Dana ↔ Renée:
   polite non-answer. Dana ↔ Tasha: "how many times?" Cliffhanger: a second school SMS — another
   pickup, today, while Dana is on shift.
2. **I Thought You Knew** — Handover argument; Marcus's slip. Tasha drafts a formal email. Notification
   Center showing Lily's synced messages to Renée. Cliffhanger: "renee pls dont tell mom ok".
3. **Thursdays** — Dana confronts Renée; Renée keeps the promise but says "please ask Lily why
   Thursdays". Jenna's message about the bus line. Dana ↔ Lily (gentle, then Lily opens up in
   fragments). Cliffhanger: Lily: "dont tell dad either. you guys will fight".
4. **Team Chat** — Dana calls a truce with Marcus; joint message to the school; Renée apology and
   boundaries; Tasha's email deleted; new group chat; Lily's closing message.

## Continuity considerations
Custody: Dana has Lily Mon–Thu on alternating weeks; the three pickups all fell on Thursdays.
Lily is in 3rd grade. Bullying is handled offscreen through the school; keep it age-appropriate,
no violence beyond taken belongings and teasing. Never depict the bullies' messages directly.
