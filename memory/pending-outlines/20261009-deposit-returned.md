# Deposit Returned

Status: pending
Proposed: 2026-10-09
Genre: Engaged-couple drama / financial deception → breakup
Setting: Melbourne, Australia; seven weeks before a wedding, present day (spring, October)
Proposed parts: 3 — a tight arc: discovery, unravelling, decision. The deception is concrete
enough that more parts would mean artificially delayed confirmation.

## Premise
Seven weeks before her wedding, a bride learns the venue deposit was refunded — to her fiancé's
account — and follows the money into a debt he has been hiding for a year.

## Synopsis
Mia gets a cheerful venue email: "Your deposit refund of $6,000 has been processed." She never
cancelled. Her fiancé Josh says it's a "banking error, I'll fix it". The venue coordinator says Josh
cancelled and rebooked a smaller midweek package. Josh's best man Kieran nervously asks Mia whether
Josh "talked to you about the thing". Through notifications on the shared iPad and a loan-provider
text, Mia uncovers sports-betting losses that have drained their joint wedding savings. Josh's sister
Lauren knew part of it. Mia must decide whether a man who lies this calmly is someone she can marry.

## Main characters
- `mia` **Mia Castellano** (31, device owner, primary-school deputy principal) — methodical, trusting
  until she isn't, doesn't raise her voice. Voice: clear, measured, emoji only when happy; goes quieter
  as she gets angrier.
- `josh` **Josh Pearce** (33, fiancé, real-estate agent) — charming, confident, a smooth lie for every
  question. Voice: "babe", "all good", fast replies, voice of reassurance; breaks into long paragraphs
  when cornered.
- `kieran` **Kieran Doyle** (33, best man) — loyal to Josh but uneasy. Voice: "mate", hedging,
  double-texts, backpedals with "ignore that".
- `lauren` **Lauren Pearce** (29, Josh's sister) — loves both of them, lent Josh $3,000 in May.
  Voice: lots of "ok so", long voice-note-like texts, ❤️.
- `stonehaven` **Stonehaven Estate Events** (venue, inbox/automated) — corporate tone.
- `coordinator` **Bec (Stonehaven coordinator)** — friendly, professional.

## Relationships
Mia and Josh: together four years, engaged 14 months, joint savings account for the wedding and a
house deposit. Kieran: Josh's friend since school. Lauren: close to Mia, often the go-between.

## Central conflict
Mia's investment in a shared future vs. mounting proof that Josh has been managing her rather than
partnering with her. The question isn't "did he do it" (that's clear by part 2) but "what does she do".

## Secrets and planned twists
- Josh cancelled the Saturday booking and pocketed the difference — Josh — venue email (part 1),
  coordinator text (part 1) — admitted part 2.
- Josh has ~$38,000 in betting losses and a personal loan — Josh — betting-app promo notification on
  the shared iPad (part 2), loan-provider overdue SMS forwarded by Lauren (part 2) — revealed part 2.
- Lauren lent Josh $3,000 and was told it was for a ring repair — Lauren — her "wait, the ring?"
  (part 2) — revealed part 2.
- Kieran knew for months — Kieran — "I told him he had to tell you" (part 3) — revealed part 3.
- No last-message twist: the turn is Mia's decision, built from evidence.

## Planned ending
Breakup with dignity. Mia cancels the wedding herself (calm emails to the venue and guests'
group chat), separates the accounts, and tells Josh she hopes he gets help but won't be the one
managing it. Final beat: a conversation-list screenshot — Josh's thread muted, Lauren's message
"still my sister. always ❤️" unread at the top. Ending type: breakup (deliberately not reconciliation).

## Part-by-part outline
1. **Refund Processed** — Lock-screen venue email notification. Mia ↔ Josh: "banking error". Mia ↔
   Bec: the rebooking to a Wednesday. Kieran's nervous text. Cliffhanger: Josh: "please don't call
   the venue again, I'm handling it" — sent *after* Mia never told him she had called.
2. **The Shared iPad** — Notification Center on the iPad: betting promos, a "Your withdrawal is
   pending" message, loan reminder. Mia ↔ Lauren: the $3,000 "ring repair". Mia ↔ Josh confrontation
   by text; he admits a "small problem" then the full number. Cliffhanger: Josh: "if you leave now
   everyone will know why."
3. **Seven Weeks** — Mia ↔ Kieran (he knew). Josh's long apology. Mia's decision. Messages to Bec
   cancelling; to the wedding-party group chat; account separation. Final conversation list.

## Continuity considerations
Fixed figures: venue deposit $6,000; joint wedding savings were $24,500, now $4,100; personal loan
$15,000; Lauren's loan $3,000. Wedding date: Saturday 28 November. Story runs 6–9 October.
Handle gambling addiction seriously — no mockery; Josh is accountable, not a cartoon villain.
