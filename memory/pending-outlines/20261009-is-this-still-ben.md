# Is This Still Ben?

Status: approved → story 0004
Proposed: 2026-10-09
Genre: Wrong-number romance / family estrangement → hopeful beginning
Setting: Tucson, Arizona; 3–21 November 2026
Proposed parts: 4. The romance needs room to grow before the estrangement complicates it. Part 3 is
Marisol hearing Ben's side, and part 4 is the fallout and a decision. Three parts would cut either
the falling-for-each-other beats or the confrontation.

## Premise
A pastry chef gets a text from a woman trying to reach her estranged brother at an old number. While
helping her find him, she falls for her, and then she finds out why he changed his number.

## Synopsis
At 11:48 pm Marisol gets a text from an unknown number: "Is this still Ben? It's Hana. I know it's been
six years." Marisol has had the number since 2024. She answers kindly, and Hana explains: she's back
in Tucson for her mother's heart surgery and wants to reach her brother before it happens. The texts
turn into late-night conversations, then coffee. Then a stranger texts the old number to congratulate
"Ben" on his bike shop, and Marisol has a lead. Hana is too scared to go, so she asks Marisol to check
quietly that he's okay. Ben works out who sent her and tells her the part Hana left out. When he came
out, their parents cut him off. Hana, who was 19 then, told him to "maybe just not make it everyone's
business". Marisol knows something Ben doesn't: Hana has never told her family she's gay either.

## Main characters
- `marisol` **Marisol Vega** (30, device owner, pastry chef at a downtown hotel, up at 4 am). She's
  open, funny, and loyal. She came out at 17 and her family were fine about it, so she doesn't always
  understand fear. Voice: lowercase, quick bursts of 2–4 messages, 🙃 and 🌵, jokes when nervous,
  goes blunt and capitalised when hurt.
- `hana` **Hana Sato** (31, structural engineer in Seattle, in Tucson for three weeks). She's careful
  and guilty, and she's out at work but not to her family. Voice: full sentences, correct punctuation,
  long messages followed by "Sorry, that was a lot."
- `ben` **Ben Sato** (36, Hana's brother, owns Saguaro Spoke bike repair). He's warm with strangers and
  shut off on this subject. He changed his number in 2020 to cut off his family. Voice: short, dry,
  no emoji, "yeah", answers questions with questions.
- `gabe` **Gabe Ortiz** (36, Ben's old college friend). He appears once and texts the old number.
  Voice: all caps excitement, "BENNY".
- `lucia` **Lucía Vega** (27, Marisol's sister, her confidant). Voice: voice-note energy, "girl.",
  🫠, nosy and supportive.

## Relationships
Marisol and Hana are strangers who grow closer: text → coffee → a first kiss offscreen at the end of
part 2. Hana and Ben haven't spoken in six years. Their parents (offscreen) cut Ben off when he came
out. Their mother, Keiko, is having bypass surgery on 18 November. Marisol and Lucía are sisters and
close.

## Central conflict
Marisol is falling for someone whose version of the past is incomplete. She's caught between honesty
with Hana, respect for Ben's right not to be found, and Hana's right to come out on her own terms.
Marisol must never out Hana to anyone.

## Secrets and planned twists
- Hana's real reason for the rift. She told Ben to keep quiet. Hana says "we drifted" (part 1). Ben
  tells Marisol the truth in part 3.
- Hana isn't out to her family. Hana reveals it to Marisol in part 2: "I've never told my parents about
  anyone." That makes Ben's account land hard in part 3.
- Ben changed his number on purpose. Gabe's text to the old number (part 2) shows that old friends
  lost him too. Ben confirms it in part 3.
- Marisol went to the shop without telling Ben who sent her. Ben's text "she sent you, didn't she" uses
  the number Marisol left on a repair ticket (part 3).
- There's no last-message twist. The turn is Hana owning what she did.

## Planned ending
Hopeful, not tidy. Hana writes Ben an apology that names what she said. Marisol passes it on only
after Ben agrees to receive it. Ben agrees to one coffee with Hana, not with their parents. Keiko's
surgery goes well. Ben doesn't visit, and Hana doesn't push. Marisol and Hana go on a real date before
Hana flies back, with a long-distance "let's see." Final beat: Marisol renames the contact
"Not Ben ❓" to "Hana 🌵". Ending type: hopeful new beginning, with the family left partly unreconciled.

## Part-by-part outline
1. **Wrong Number** (3–5 Nov). Lock-screen notification at 11:48 pm: "Is this still Ben?" Marisol ↔
   Hana: polite correction, then Hana's careful explanation ("we drifted"). Marisol ↔ Lucía: "a
   stranger is trauma-dumping and I think I like her??" Second night: their texts get longer, a
   timestamp jumps to 2:10 am, and Marisol has a 4 am shift. Cliffhanger: Hana: "Could I buy you a
   coffee? I'd like to know what a person who answers wrong numbers looks like."
2. **Saguaro Spoke** (7–12 Nov). After the coffee (timestamp gap), the texts are warmer. Hana tells
   Marisol she's never told her parents about anyone. Gabe's all-caps text to the old number arrives:
   "BENNY saw Saguaro Spoke in the Daily Star!!!" Marisol ↔ Hana: there's a lead, and Hana is
   frightened. She asks Marisol to "just see if he's okay. Don't say my name." Lucía: "this is a
   terrible idea, I'm in." Cliffhanger: Marisol books a tune-up at the shop for Saturday.
3. **Repair Ticket** (14–15 Nov). Marisol ↔ Lucía live-texting from the shop. That evening an unknown
   number texts: "you're the woman with the green Raleigh. she sent you, didn't she." Marisol ↔ Ben:
   he isn't cruel, just clear. He tells her what Hana said when he came out. Marisol says nothing about
   Hana. Marisol ↔ Hana: "you didn't drift. you told him to keep it quiet." Cliffhanger: Hana's typing
   indicator, then it stops, and the read receipt sits with no reply.
4. **Hana 🌵** (16–21 Nov). Hana's long reply: no excuses, and the link to her own closet. Marisol
   refuses to be a go-between without Ben's consent and asks him first. Ben: "one message. then we
   see." Hana's apology goes through Marisol, word for word. Surgery-day notification Center: a
   hospital update from Hana, Lucía checking in. Ben: "tell her saturday, 10, the shop. just her." A
   date. The final screenshot is the contact rename and the conversation list.

## Continuity considerations
Fixed facts: Marisol has had the number since spring 2024. Ben changed his number in March 2020. Keiko
Sato's bypass surgery is Wed 18 Nov at Banner–University Medical Center Tucson. Hana flies to Seattle
on Sun 22 Nov. Marisol's bike is a green Raleigh. Keep the coming-out themes serious and specific:
parents are offstage and not cartoon villains, and nobody is outed by anyone else.

## Originality assessment
Compared with 0001 The Rent Went Up, 0002 Pickup at 3:15 and 0003 Deposit Returned, plus the two other
proposals in this batch ("Split the Rental", "Before 8 A.M.").
- It's the only story where the device owner is a stranger to the central family, and the only one
  with a sapphic romance.
- The secret is a past act of complicity, not money, cheating or a child's problem.
- The evidence comes from a recycled number (a stranger's misdirected text and a repair ticket), not
  from automated venue or bank messages.
- The ending is a new beginning, which no existing story uses.
- Ben's need for privacy is a deliberate counterweight, so the plot isn't "find the lost relative and
  everyone hugs".
